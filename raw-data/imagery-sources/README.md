# Imagery Sources

This folder tracks candidate imagery sources for identifying and characterizing data centers from remote sensing. We are starting with Illinois and can expand the same format to additional states as the project grows.

## Illinois

| Source | Coverage | Resolution | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cook County High Resolution Imagery | Cook County, Illinois | 6 inch | <https://hub-cookcountyil.opendata.arcgis.com/pages/imagery> | Annual aerial imagery for Cook County. Useful for detailed review in the Chicago metro area. |
| NAIP | Illinois statewide | 60 cm | <https://nrcs.app.box.com/v/naip/folder/364773194011> | Public aerial imagery covering all of Illinois. Useful statewide baseline where higher-resolution county imagery is unavailable. |
| Earth Genome Sentinel-2 Composites | Global, including Illinois | 10 m | <https://source.coop/earthgenome/earthindeximagery> | Annual Sentinel-2 composites. Useful for broad screening, temporal context, and land-cover signals rather than fine site details. |
| IDOT 2011 Orthophotography | Illinois statewide | 1 ft | <https://clearinghouse.isgs.illinois.edu/data> | 2011 orthophotography available through the Illinois Geospatial Data Clearinghouse. Useful historical baseline. |
| Vantor via SkyFi | Commercial tasking or archive; available for Illinois areas of interest | 30 cm / 1 ft | <https://app.skyfi.com/> | Commercial imagery option. Current working estimate is $20/km² for 30 cm imagery. |
